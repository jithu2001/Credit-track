From the Tally PC to the field, without anyone exporting a report

A small Windows program sits next to TallyPrime. Every few minutes it reads what changed and sends it to your own database. The apps read from there.

Dues

Who owes what, grouped the way you collect

Every shop with a balance, highest first, grouped by site, with a total for each site. Each shop's balance is the same as its ledger in Tally, to the paisa. Tap a phone number to call or WhatsApp the shop.

Overdue

Overdue past your credit days, not just "unpaid"

Choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 days. Each receipt clears the oldest bill first, so you see exactly which bills are late and by how many days, shop by shop.

Statement

The ledger, with a running balance

Sales, receipts and credit notes for any shop, newest first, each with the balance after it. If the bills don't add up to Tally's balance the app says so instead of hiding it.

Stock

Stock without phoning the godown

Closing quantity and value for every item, with low and out-of-stock filters. Purchases and suppliers are there for you; the Staff app shows salesmen quantities only, not your purchase rates.

Sites

Your routes, not Tally's groups

Group shops into sites the way your salesmen actually travel: Kumily, Kattappana, Adimali. Each site shows outstanding, sales and collections for the month.

Common questions

Does WholeFlow change anything in my Tally data?

No. WholeFlow only reads from TallyPrime. Every request it sends to Tally is checked against a read-only list before it leaves the PC, and anything that could write is refused.

Which Tally version do I need?

TallyPrime on a Windows PC, with Client/Server set to Server (or Both). Your company has to be open in Tally while it syncs.

What happens when the Tally PC is switched off?

The phones keep showing the last synced figures with the time they were updated. When the PC and Tally are back, WholeFlow catches up on everything changed in the meantime. Nothing is deleted while Tally is offline.

Can a salesman see every shop?

Only if you allow it. You give each staff member a whole company or just the sites (groups of shops) they cover, and choose whether they can see bills and receipts. The server enforces this, not just the app.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. The Owner and Staff apps are for Android phones.
